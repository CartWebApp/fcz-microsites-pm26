Physical-profile

Size range
Males measure 8.5-10 feet (2.6-3m) and females measuring 7.5-9 (2.3-2.7m).
Weight
Males weigh 330-550 lbs (150-250 kg) Females are smaller, weighing 265-395 lbs (120-180 kg)
Identification Markings
Identification Markings: Light buff to tawny-brown coats with white underparts. Adults have a black tuft at the end of their tail. Adult males possess a prominent mane that darkens with age, ranging from blonde to jet black. Cubs are born with dark rosettes/spots that fade as they mature. [2]

Habitat & Geography

Habitats: Grasslands, savannas, dense scrub and open woodlands. Distribution: Primarily restricted to fragmented pockets of Sub-Saharan Africa.
