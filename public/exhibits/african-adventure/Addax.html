Distribution Map

Ecology and Behavior

Feeding and Diet

Herbivores, feeding on shrubs, leaves, and succulents.

Communication and Social Behavior

Addax are nomadic and travel long distances to places with recent rainfall. They communicate using scent, grunts, snorts, and alarm calls.

Life Cycle and Breeding Behaviors

Addax have an 8-9 month gestation period and live in nomadic groups.

Predators

Natural predators of the Addax include lions, cheetahs, and hyenas.
