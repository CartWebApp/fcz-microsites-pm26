Galapagos Tortoise

Description

Conservation

The conservation status of these Galapago Tortoise according to the IUCN Red List is listed as an endangered species. Regarding population, there isaround 20,000/25,000 tortoise left in the world.

Behavior

The tortoise's lifespan is somewhere around 170+ years. They live a relatively peaceful life, napping about 16 hours per day. The rest of the time is spent foraging on leaves, cactus and mainly basking in the sun.

Habitat

Native Ecuador

Average Size: Sizes up 4 to 6 feet Lifespan: Typically live up to 177 years of life Diet: These Herbivores eat vegetation including grasses, cactus and leaves Clutch Size: A clutch size for these tortoises are 2-7 eggs per lay

Population Status: 15,000 to 20,000 individuals remaining in the wild Other Names: Chelonoidis niger Geochelone nigra
