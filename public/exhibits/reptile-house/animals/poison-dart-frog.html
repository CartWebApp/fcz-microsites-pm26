Poison Dart Frog

Description

Males of this species have a wide sagittal red stripe with a blue dot on its muzzle. Females have a dark gray head with a blurred orange stripe and a brownish-gray carapace.

Conservation

The Painted Terrapin is listed as critically endangered by the IUCN. To help conserve these turtles there are annual nesting patrol to secure eggs from poaching, head starting, releasing, field monitoring, habitat restoration, environmental education have been conducted.

Behavior

They are usually solitary. They are nocturnal. Their only known behaviors that are recorded is mating, feeding and basking. They can be seen on logs basking in the sun. They are also aggressive and will try to bite if you try and pick them up.

Habitat

Found in the Southeast Asia in the Mangrove biomes.

Average Size: Female: 20-28 inches Males: 12-16 inches Lifespan: 40-80 years Diet: Fruits, shoots, and aquatic plants Clutch Size: 12-20 eggs

Incubation: 70-90 days Sexual Maturity: 6-8 years Predators: There are no known natural predators
